# data_analysis_technical_assessment
Data Analyst Technical Assessment. 
Data_Analysis_Technical_Assessment
Objective
Analyze monthly performance for Branches A, B, and C using sales, operating cost, and customer feedback data.

Calculations
•	Net Profit = Total Sales - Operating Cost
•	Profit Margin % = Net Profit / Total Sales
•	Customer sentiment is ranked from 1 (best) to 3 based on the supplied feedback.
•	A column chart compares Net Profit across the three branches.

Result 
Branch	Sales	Operating Cost	Net Profit	Profit Margin	 Sentiment Rank
Branch A	$45,000	$32,000	$13,000	28.89%	2
Branch B	$38,500	$21,000	$17,500	45.45%	1
Branch C	$52,000	$48,000	  $4,000	7.69%		3

Manager Pitch
Branch B should receive the Performance Bonus because it has the highest net profit of $17,500, the highest profit margin of 45.45%, and the best customer feedback - always has stock and friendly staff. Branch C should receive a Progress Improvement plan because, notwithstanding it generated the highest sales of $52,000, its net profit of $4,000 and profit margin of 7.69% are much lower than the other two branches; it also has a negative customer review - expensive shipping and slow delivery. Branch A is profitable with a $13,000 net profit, but its long queues as reviewed by a customer, suggest an opportunity to improve service speed and customer flow.   

Bonus Challenge
Branch C's original operating cost is $48,000.
15% cost reduction: $48,000 × 15% = $7,200
New operating cost: $48,000 - $7,200 = $40,800
New net profit: $52,000 - $40,800 = $11,200
New profit margin: $11,200 / $52,000 = 21.54%
